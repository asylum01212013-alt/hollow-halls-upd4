HOLLOW HALLS

Seven places that should have stayed sealed. Find a way out of each one. Headphones strongly recommended.

1 · THE ASYLUM

Find the 5 relics, then reach the exit sigil. It hunts by sight: walls hide you, sprinting and your flashlight give you away.

2 · THE SEWERS

A giant spider that lunges. Pull all 4 levers to open the gate. Run or sidestep when it coils.

3 · THE FACILITY

Press all 6 buttons to unlock the doors. Listen for the chainsaw.

4 · THE OCEAN

Find 6 submarine fragments in the dark, then hold still while the sub is repaired. Your lamp draws the leviathan.

5 · THE FOREST

Find the exit. The clock-headed thing is blind but hears you. Moving and your microphone make noise.

6 · THE SUPERCENTER

Press 10 buttons in the memorized order. Mannequins freeze while you watch them.

7 · THE PYRAMID

Solve 3 puzzles. Three mistakes are allowed; the fourth brings the chanting. The queen is the fastest thing alive.
